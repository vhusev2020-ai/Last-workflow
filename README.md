# VCORP UNIVERSE
### A living world of ideas, ventures, and intelligent agents

> Build intelligently. Grow organically. Preserve what we learn. Share what works.

VCorp Universe is an evolving ecosystem where ideas become experiments, experiments become projects, and successful projects may become ventures of their own. Knowledge and useful resources flow back into the wider universe.

## The living structure
- **Intelligence Core** — coordination, strategy, memory, and decisions.
- **Ventures** — businesses and revenue-generating projects.
- **Labs** — research, prototypes, and experiments.
- **Technology** — software, AI, and automation.
- **Operations** — practical execution and delivery.
- **Capital** — budgets, resources, and reinvestment.
- **Knowledge** — institutional memory and shared learning.

These are functions, not rigid departments or necessarily separate legal entities. They can change as the universe grows.
