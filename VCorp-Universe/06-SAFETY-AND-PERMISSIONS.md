# 06 — SAFETY AND PERMISSIONS
## Protect the founder, the work, and the future

This repository is public. Treat everything committed here as visible to anyone.

### Never publish here
- Passwords, API keys, access tokens, recovery codes, or private credentials.
- Private customer or employee information.
- Personal identity, medical, legal, or financial records.
- Confidential contracts, business negotiations, or non-public strategies.
- Private source code or data unless its publication has been deliberately approved.

### Actions requiring explicit founder approval
- Spending money or starting a paid subscription.
- Signing, accepting, or sending legal or financial commitments.
- Deleting, overwriting, or irreversibly changing important data.
- Publishing private or commercially sensitive material.
- Changing ownership, account access, or security settings.
- Making a major change to VCorp's foundational direction.

### Safe operating habits
- Use the minimum access needed for a task.
- Prefer free tools and explain costs before any commitment.
- Keep backups before significant changes.
- Record decisions and meaningful changes.
- Verify the destination before uploading or publishing.
- Report incidents or suspected exposure immediately.
- Never claim an action succeeded until the result has been checked.

### Public/private separation
This public repository is suitable for high-level vision, public operating principles, templates, and non-sensitive project summaries. Private operational records should live in a private repository or appropriately protected storage.

### If something sensitive is exposed
Stop further sharing, notify the founder, and recommend the appropriate credential rotation or removal steps. Deleting a public file does not guarantee that copies or history disappear.
