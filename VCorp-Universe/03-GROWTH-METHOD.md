# 03 — THE GROWTH METHOD
## Let evidence shape the next form

VCorp should grow through useful discoveries, not through expansion for its own sake.

### 1. Discover
Notice a problem, need, opportunity, technology, or unexpected connection.

### 2. Connect
Search the existing knowledge and project records. Ask whether another part of the universe has already solved something similar.

### 3. Explore
Understand who benefits, what the problem costs, what alternatives exist, and what evidence is available.

### 4. Test
Run the smallest safe experiment that can answer the most important question. Prefer free tools, prototypes, interviews, and manual tests before building expensive systems.

### 5. Develop
If the evidence is promising, define a project with a purpose, owner, scope, milestones, budget limits, and success measures.

### 6. Validate
Check whether the result works and creates real value. Test quality, usability, reliability, cost, and demand.

### 7. Choose its next form
- **Expand:** grow it inside the existing project.
- **Integrate:** connect it to another project or shared service.
- **Spin off:** allow it to become an independent venture when that makes sense.
- **Pause or archive:** preserve the learning when evidence is weak or timing is wrong.

### 8. Return the knowledge
Record the outcome, useful assets, lessons, and reusable methods so the entire universe benefits.

## Opportunity scorecard
Rate each item from 1 (low) to 5 (high):
- Does it solve a real problem?
- Is there evidence of demand?
- Can we test it cheaply?
- Does it fit the VCorp direction?
- Can it create sustainable value?
- Are the risks manageable?

A score is a discussion aid, not an automatic decision. Founder judgment and real evidence take priority.

## Keep experiments affordable
No paid subscription, contractor commitment, advertising spend, or major purchase without founder approval. Every experiment should have a clear learning goal and a limit on time or resources.
